## Product profile

I am a fourth year Computer Science student at An Najah National University building my career in Product Management and Product Ownership.

I enjoy understanding why a problem matters, deciding what should be built first, and turning rough ideas into clear work a team can deliver. My strongest work sits between user needs, business goals, and technical execution.

In 2026, I earned three consecutive hackathon awards while working across product and technical teams. I led Wafeer as Project Manager, shaped the product direction for Voice of the Market, and led the technical delivery of MIN HON.

On August 31, 2026, I will begin Voyage 62 as a Product Owner Intern with Chingu, working with a remote team through a complete Agile product cycle.

I am open to Product Management, Associate Product Manager, and Product Owner internship opportunities.

## Product portfolio

### [Voice of the Market](https://github.com/yousefwalidabubaker/voice-of-the-market-ai)

An AI driven retail availability system that combines in fridge inventory monitoring with customer QR reports. It classifies and prioritizes alerts so Siniora and market teams can respond faster.

**Result:** Winner, Voice of the Market Challenge at AINAK Challenges 2026  
**Team:** The Executive Congress  
**My contribution:** Problem framing, product direction, MVP scope, user flows, feature priorities, backlog, acceptance criteria, product metrics, and pitch narrative

The repository documents the product vision, PRD, roadmap, prioritized backlog, experiments, risks, assumptions, and success metrics.

### [Wafeer](WAFEER_PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md)

Wafeer connects two products. The customer app helps people find safe products approaching their expiry date at lower prices. Wafeer Partners helps stores manage inventory, apply timely discounts, and offer eligible food to verified charities while it is still safe, edible, and within its valid date.

**Result:** Potential to Scale Award, Global TechBridge Hackathon in Amman, 2026  
**Role:** Project Manager  
**My contribution:** User definition, value proposition, feature priorities, team coordination, product story, pitch, and demo  
**Experience:** Training and mentorship from Microsoft engineers

I created the [product backlog](WAFEER_PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md) and [product board](WAFEER_PRODUCT_BOARD.md), with each story documented as a GitHub Issue including its priority, dependencies, acceptance criteria, tasks, edge cases, and test criteria.

### [MIN HON](https://github.com/yousefwalidabubaker/MIN-HON)

A bilingual shopping experience inspired by Palestinian heritage, with product customization and an AI shopping assistant.

**Result:** Second Place, Innovate IT Hackathon AI Track  
**Role:** Technical Team Lead  
**My contribution:** Product execution, team coordination, AI integration, shopping flow, customization experience, and final pitch

## How I approach product work

* Start with the user problem before discussing features
* Define the Product Goal and what success should look like
* Prioritize the smallest useful MVP
* Turn requirements into clear user stories and acceptance criteria
* Keep product, design, and technical work connected
* Use feedback and evidence to improve the next decision
